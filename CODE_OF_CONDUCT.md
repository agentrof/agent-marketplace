# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, releases and every
repository-maintained communication channel.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and respect for other people.
- Challenge ideas directly without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable and proportionate to the change.
- Protect private information, credentials and security-sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive review, issue or automation behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a conduct or security concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request a correction, remove content, limit participation or
block access when this policy is breached. Use GitHub's report controls or
contact a repository administrator privately for conduct concerns. Report
security vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
