# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, releases and every
repository-maintained communication channel.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and respect for other people.
- Challenge ideas directly without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable and proportionate to the change.
- Protect private information, credentials and security-sensitive details.
- Keep requests, reviews and automated activity within the agreed scope.

## AI-assisted participation

Contributors remain responsible for the code, text and automated actions they
submit. Review generated contributions before publishing. State what was actually
tested, distinguish observations from assumptions and disclose material gaps.
Fabricated test results, source citations or review evidence undermine the project.
Use bounded automation and avoid duplicate issues, unsolicited bulk changes or
repeated messages that overwhelm maintainers. Follow the
[contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for technical review and verification.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive review, issue or automation behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a conduct or security concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request a correction, remove content, limit participation or
block access when this policy is breached. Reports should describe the conduct
and include the minimum relevant context while protecting private information.
Use GitHub's report controls or contact a repository administrator privately for
conduct concerns. Report security vulnerabilities through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
